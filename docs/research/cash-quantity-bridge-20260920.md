# Returned-activity cash and quantity bridge — 20 September 2026

`alphaos/reports/cash_quantity_bridge.py` adds pure diagnostic accounting between two archived adjacent-session common-valuation captures. It recomputes both endpoint bundles, verifies saved input receipts and matching account/feed, and requires the starting capture before the next endpoint. It does not fetch, persist, schedule, activate a method or change trading behavior.

The bridge independently accounts for signed share movements and cash: purchases reduce cash; sales add cash; dividends, fees and interest retain their individual movements; external deposits and withdrawals remain separate even when their net amount is zero. Cash must reconcile within one cent and quantities within1e-9 shares. It exposes residuals and exact activity-input hashes. The supported success state is `returned_activities_reconciled`, not a certified investment return. The dollar wealth change excludes observed external net inflows; it is not a time-weighted return or a net-of-operating-cost profit measure.

Review reproduced a creation-time/transaction-time mistake: two old offsetting activity receipts could change amounts without invalidating the bridge when their transaction times preceded the later query's start. The fix compares every shared receipt regardless of transaction time and refuses missing previous IDs as a coverage-review requirement. A rolling creation-time query cannot prove why an old transaction disappeared. A wider query or independent source coverage is needed to resolve that case; transaction dates cannot silently excuse it.

Tests cover partial exits, flat holdings, cash/quantity discrepancies, offsetting transfers, late old events, changed/missing historical receipts, tampered derived marks, mismatched account/feed and late starting captures. An independent review additionally checked signed fractional short covers and exact start/end boundaries. The affected selection passed67 tests; the independent11-check script and evidence remain in the repair workspace.

`comparison_eligible` is always false. The bridge does not prove absence of omitted offsetting activities, verify broker statements/late postings, add dividend reinvestment to SPY, account for subscription/API costs, or activate a research contract. M1 remains partial until those upstream and downstream commitments are completed with real endpoint pairs.
